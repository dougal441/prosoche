<objective>
Discriminate, at rung 2 of the evidence ladder, which action identifier inside the Mirror
primitive's span carries the inherited axis-4 unfilled-required-picker defect — the one that raises
*"Please choose a value for each parameter in this action."* and stops Circle 7, and therefore
Circle 8, from running on a phone.

Purpose: this defect is device-reproduced three times across two independent installs and is proven
to follow the **primitive**, not the Circle index. Circle 8 currently *is* that primitive, so
whatever Phase 15 ships at Circle 8 inherits it. `15-RESEARCH.md` narrows the suspects to exactly
three identifiers by exonerating everything Circles 1, 3 and 9 exercised on device. Doing the
Circle-8 rewrite blind guarantees a rework, which is why the discrimination belongs in this phase
even if the fix does not (D-04).
Output: a signed, alert-free probe; a measured verdict; and a routed decision.

**Rung discipline (`.claude/CLAUDE.md` §9, binding).** This is a **rung-2** question: no Notes app,
no Apple Intelligence, no Personal Automations, no real-hardware brightness or volume — none of
rung 2's ceiling applies. Do **not** request a device session for it. Never climb higher than the
open question requires, and never skip a rung that would have caught a defect in the probe itself.

**This plan modifies no generator, no fork and no checker.** It is disjoint from plan 15-01 by
construction so the two run in the same wave.
</objective>
