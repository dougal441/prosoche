<objective>
Implement locked decision **D-01**: `safety.brightness_floor` moves `0.10 -> 0` and
`safety.dim_target` moves `0.12 -> 0`. Dimming reaches the device's true minimum.

The correction has to be observable rather than inert. A floor of 0 under an unchanged target of
0.12 would never bind, so the target follows the floor down - that is why D-01 moves both.

The real work is that "never zero" is encoded in **six** places, and this is one defect class. The
project rule is fix whole classes, never site-by-site. Two of the six are requirement statements
that are now literally false, and they get amended with a visible trace to D-01, not quietly
rewritten.

The safety property is unchanged and is the whole point of the phase: **capture-and-restore
reliability**, not floor avoidance. Plan 16-01 made that property real for the first time by
persisting the capture; this plan is only safe to land after it, which is why it is wave 2.

Purpose: retire a clause the evidence retired, on the main line, without weakening anything that is
still load-bearing.

Output: both forks rebuilt with the new Config literal; two checkers reconciled; four records
amended.

**Constraint D-MANIFEST:** `python3 docs/manifest_check.py` remains deliberately red from plan
16-01's first rebuild until plan 16-05 re-signs and refreshes it. Do NOT edit MANIFEST rows.
</objective>
