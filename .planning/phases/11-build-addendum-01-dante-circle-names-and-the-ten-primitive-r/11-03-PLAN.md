<objective>
Rename the Apple Note to the bare product name, surface Dante's nine Circle names, and add the
optional hardening instruction — the three remaining user-facing halves of Build Addendum 01
§1, §3 and §4.

The Note is the product's entire control surface. Three separate strings decide its identity:
the predicate that finds it, the H1 a person reads, and the title the create action writes. All
three are user-facing and all three move. Every one of them sits upstream of the body's two
attachment placeholders, so every one of them shifts absolute offsets that can crash Shortcuts
on import if they are not recomputed.

Two collisions are handled here deliberately rather than discovered on a device. First, the
shortened title makes the existing "name contains" lookup less specific — the operator stays as
it is, because it is a recorded decision backed by the documented Find-Notes name-matching trap
and there is no device evidence for the alternative, so the collision is mitigated in copy and
recorded as a deviation instead. Second, `Limbo` is already the middle profile name and BD-06
also makes it Circle 1's name; the copy disambiguates rather than renaming either.

Purpose: land the user-facing half of the addendum once, with the offset invariant armed.
Output: a note titled with the bare product name, a stable nine-Circle legend, an optional
hardening section, and both forks re-signed and decrypt-verified.
</objective>
