<objective>
Close the guard that could not fail, raise two floors that had no teeth, and put the interim
record where the phase's own prohibition requires it.

`verify_panic_escape_seed()`'s third assertion matches conditionals by a bare variable-name
literal that exists nowhere as a shared constant — the emitter carries its own copy forty lines
away. The verifier ran the precise negative control: rename only the emitter's variable and flip
its gate to the exact existence test the guard forbids, leave the guard's literal untouched, and
the build exits 0 with all twelve checkers green. The guard is vacuous under exactly the drift it
exists to catch, and it does not cover the two gates that decide whether the flag is written at
all.

Two more guards read stronger than they are. The token-string floor sits roughly a third below
the measured value, leaving hundreds of units of slack on the axis-2 defect it names. The Phase 5
brightness assertion compares a value that is a dictionary by construction against three scalars,
so it has never had the capacity to fail.

And the phase prohibited presenting an interim stand-in as designed behaviour, requiring each to
be named in the generator **and** in `docs/BUILD-NOTES.md` with its replacing phase. Both are
recorded — in the generator and in the config block. Neither is in the file the prohibition names.

Purpose: leave behind guards that fail on the defects they name, and a record where it was asked
to be.
Output: one guard resolved by provenance, two floors moved to measured values, and two interim
records in the required file.
</objective>
