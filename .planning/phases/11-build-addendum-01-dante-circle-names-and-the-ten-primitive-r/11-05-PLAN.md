<objective>
Make Panic Escape deliberately removable, per Build Addendum 01 §3, without weakening a single
safety mechanism.

Panic Escape is the `Leaving` option in `universal_leaving()` — the easy behavioural bypass
offered before every primitive in every sequence and Circle. It is **not** Emergency Restore,
which is a MANUAL menu item and one of the two options inside the live-cooldown redirect, and
which stays unconditionally available. Conflating the two is the failure this plan is written to
prevent: one is a comfort the user may choose to give up, the other is the thing that gets them
out of a state they cannot otherwise leave.

Removal is deliberately hard. The user edits a setting line in the Note by hand, then confirms
explicitly. Nothing about it is a single tap, and the same route restores it.

Purpose: give the user a real, reversible way to remove their own escape hatch, and prove
mechanically that the safety hatch is untouched.
Output: `panic_escape_enabled` as a first-class state field, a gated bypass menu, a MANUAL
removal-and-restore path, and two site-count tables moved with rewritten derivations.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**The transition.** Panic Escape moves from unconditionally present to deliberately removable.
An advisory assumption-delta scan flagged a `required -> optional` transition in this phase's
scope; the literal snippet it matched is a prose false positive, but the underlying transition
is real and is answered here.

**The noun that is now primary.** The *removable behavioural bypass*, represented as the
first-class state field `panic_escape_enabled`. The `Leaving` menu case is one rendering of that
noun, not the noun itself.

**The decision: `promote`.** `panic_escape_enabled` becomes the primary representation and the
single source of truth for whether the bypass is offered; "the Leaving/Continue menu is always
emitted" is demoted to a detail of the `panic_escape_enabled > 0` variant. This is a promotion,
not an add-alongside: there is exactly one place that decides whether the bypass exists, and
`universal_leaving()` reads it rather than assuming it.

**Rationale.** One source of truth, one gate, no second flag to drift. The alternative —
emitting the menu unconditionally and suppressing it somewhere downstream — would leave two
places that decide the same thing and would break the structural guarantee that exactly one
`["Leaving","Continue"]` menu exists inside the silent band.

**Explicitly not conflated.** Emergency Restore is a *safety mechanism*, not a variant of the
bypass. It is not represented by this field, is not gated by it, and no conditional introduced
by this plan encloses it. The two nouns stay separate at every level: state, control flow, and
copy.
</assumption_delta_decision>
