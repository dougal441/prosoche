<objective>
Ship the two variants under their new names — and close the one defect this phase inherited by
name.

A signed `.shortcut` carries no display name inside it. The signer strips `WFWorkflowName` from
the payload; the AEA1 auth data holds only the certificate chain. Measured by decryption. The
filename is therefore the sole carrier of the name a user sees in their library, and the user's
two Personal Automations reference that library entry by name. A rename here is not cosmetic: it
is a breaking change for anyone who already imported the old build, and there is no API that can
patch their automations.

The inherited defect: the Aware fork's Control Room Note currently names the *other* fork's
shortcut in both of its automation sections, and reports the other fork's label in its settings
block. It was recorded as deferred and explicitly assigned to this phase. Deferring it again
means shipping a build that instructs its users to select a shortcut that will not exist under
the new names.

Purpose: land the rename once, everywhere it is load-bearing, and prove it reached the signed
artifacts.
Output: two renamed, validated, signed and decrypt-verified deliverables, a refreshed manifest,
and a written breaking-change record.
</objective>
