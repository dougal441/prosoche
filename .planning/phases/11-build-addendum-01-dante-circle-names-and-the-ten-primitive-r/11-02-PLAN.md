<objective>
Make the dispatch surface impossible to break silently, then move the whole roster onto it in
one commit.

BD-06 Decision 5 is the operative instruction: every distinct primitive name in any
`sequences` array must have exactly one dispatch branch, and every branch must be named by at
least one sequence. That guard is written **during** the rename, not after it, because a mass
rename across three arrays and ten branch renderings is precisely the operation it exists to
catch, and this defect class is invisible to the validator, to the ToolKit catalog, and to the
signed-artifact decrypt. Circle 8 has shipped dead for four phases for exactly this reason.

The condition 99 -> 4 move is a correctness prerequisite, not cleanup. Under "contains", the
new Circle-8 entry `Loud Mirror` also matches the `Mirror` branch, so it is undispatchable as
a distinct primitive until dispatch is exact. The three combined entries that were the only
reason condition 99 was ever used are abolished by BD-06 in the same move.

Purpose: turn a silent runtime no-op into a build-time failure, permanently.
Output: nine live primitive names, ninety exact-match dispatch conditionals, a hard build
guard in both builders, and a twelfth-plus-one checker that gates instead of reporting.
</objective>
