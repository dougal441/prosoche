<objective>
Make Circle 2 do something to the phone.

Today `ash()` is a comment plus one alert — that alert **is** Circle 2, and on device Circle 2
and Circle 1 are two alerts with different words. This plan replaces it with the real thing:
the donor-confirmed Color Filters action on, nothing else; and one unconditional off, placed
where four separate recovery paths already run.

**The insight that shrank this phase: grayscale has exactly two values, so there is nothing to
remember.** Brightness needs a capture because its original is one of a continuum. Colour does
not. "Restore" is unconditionally "set it off", which is why there is no snapshot group, no
ownership marker, no persist-before-apply ordering, and no read-back probe in this plan. The
whole capture-and-restore machine that surrounds `dimming()` and `silence()` is deliberately
NOT extended to this primitive, and the guards are registered — or deliberately not registered —
on that basis rather than by analogy.

Purpose: CIRC-02's audited visual-salience reduction becomes real, and SAFE-05's promise to
restore colour settings — which the requirement text has always made and the build has never
delivered — becomes true.

Output: a fork whose Circle 2 turns the screen black and white and whose CLOSE, Emergency
Restore, Ice expiry and live-Ice redirect all turn it back.
</objective>
